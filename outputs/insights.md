# Key insights (sample data)

## Diabetes

- The diabetes market is worth ₹180.9 Cr in 2025, growing 7.4% vs 2024. Glucora leads with 28.4% share.
- Dapaflo is the fastest share-gainer (Evolution Index 121): it grew 30.0% against market growth of 7.4%.
- Sitavia looks healthy on sales (+6.2%) but its EI is 98.9, so it is quietly losing share. Recommend reviewing positioning against faster-growing brands.
- Glucora is declining in absolute sales (-2.1%, EI 91).
- Glimex is declining in absolute sales (-7.3%, EI 86).
- Empagen has one of the highest prescriber penetrations (42.8%) but only 14.2% share: many doctors try it but prescribe low volumes. Opportunity: deepen usage among existing prescribers.
- For Dapaflo, penetration is highest in North (46.7%) and lowest in East (24.8%), the clearest region to target next.

## Hypertension

- The hypertension market is worth ₹153.7 Cr in 2025, growing 3.0% vs 2024. Cardiva leads with 31.0% share.
- Olmitra is the fastest share-gainer (Evolution Index 113): it grew 16.2% against market growth of 3.0%.
- Amlocor is declining in absolute sales (-3.7%, EI 93).
- Rosartan is declining in absolute sales (-6.2%, EI 91).
- Olmitra has one of the highest prescriber penetrations (36.9%) but only 15.7% share: many doctors try it but prescribe low volumes. Opportunity: deepen usage among existing prescribers.
- For Olmitra, penetration is highest in East (48.8%) and lowest in North (27.2%), the clearest region to target next.
